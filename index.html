Fab-Top Tables  
Work table for pipefitters, plumbers, electricians, welders; mounts on tripod vise

       
A valuable tool for the pipefitter, plumber, electrician or welder giving much-needed additional workspace. The table provides a 24" x 27" work surface of strong cast aluminum, weighing less than 30 pounds. Mounts on top of a tripod pipe vise (like Ridgid). Installing quickly with one 5/8" bolt, nut and washer (included); does not interfere with the operation of the vise. Very useful addition to the tools or equipment for pipefitting, plumbing, steamfitting, welding and electrical work. 
Designed by a journeyman pipefitter. Fab-top Tables have been sold throughout the US since 1994.
